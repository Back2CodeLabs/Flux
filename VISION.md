# Vision

Project F.L.U.X. exists to simplify life.

Not by replacing humans.

By empowering them.

---

Our goal is to create a team of AI specialists.

Not one giant assistant.

Each agent has a mission.

Each mission has a purpose.

---

We believe:

Simple beats complex.

Open beats proprietary.

Local beats cloud.

Architecture beats improvisation.

Documentation beats assumptions.

---

Five years from now,

we want Project F.L.U.X. to remain understandable,

maintainable,

and enjoyable to use.

That is success.
